[CAMERA — OPENING RECOMMENDATION]

I recommend WATCH AND DEFER,
with zero allocation,
to Cadence Design Systems
at the September first,
twenty-twenty-six closing price
of three hundred thirteen dollars
and four cents.

The company is strong,
but the current price requires
more than the evidence supports.

The integrated F-C-F-F D-C-F
produces a range of
fifty-seven dollars and twenty cents
to one hundred eleven dollars
and fifty-two cents per share,
with a base value
of eighty-three dollars
and thirty-four cents.

//

[SHOW TOP OF WORKBENCH]

At the top of the workbench,
the committee action comes
from the calculated value.

The five-year statements
connect to F-C-F-F,
and the bridge converts
twenty-two-point-two-five-seven billion dollars
of enterprise value
into twenty-two-point-seven-seven-eight billion
of equity value—

or eighty-three dollars
and thirty-four cents per share.

All statement
and cash-flow checks pass.

//

[SHOW VALUATION COMPARISON]

I do not average the D-C-F
with the peer methods.

Synopsys implies
one hundred eighty-nine dollars
and sixty-four cents
from E-V to revenue,

and two hundred nine dollars
and forty-seven cents
from P-to-E.

Both remain below
three hundred thirteen dollars
and four cents.

I then challenged
my unusually conservative D-C-F
against outside estimates.

Value Investing dot I-O
reported one hundred thirty-seven dollars
and thirty-seven cents.

Fair Value Calculator
reported two hundred twenty-four dollars
and twenty-four cents.

And Alpha Spread reported
two hundred seventy-two dollars
and ninety-two cents.

These were post-date checks—
not inputs to my
September first decision.

//

[SHOW ALPHA SPREAD RECONCILIATION]

Alpha Spread
is the optimistic boundary.

Its published case uses
seven years of F-C-F-E,

net margin ending near
thirty-four percent,

one-hundred-one-percent
cash conversion,

a nine-point-three-percent
discount rate,

and a twenty-nine-point-one-times
terminal P-to-E.

Applying those assumptions
to roughly
five-point-eight-three-eight billion dollars
of starting revenue
produces two hundred seventy-seven dollars
and forty-six cents
in my reconstruction—

close to Alpha Spread's value.

My model uses
five years of accounting-linked F-C-F-F,
a ten-percent WACC,
three-percent perpetual growth,
and net margins reaching
roughly twenty percent.

The gap is mainly
the margin
and terminal-value philosophy—
not hidden arithmetic.

Even the optimistic outside value
remains below the market.

//

[CHANGE BASE TO DOWNSIDE]

The decision engine changes
with the calculated output—
not the scenario name.

When I switch from Base
to Downside,
value falls to
fifty-seven dollars and twenty cents,
and the action changes
to DO NOT INITIATE.

[RETURN TO BASE]

I will return to Base
for the remaining analysis.

//

[SHOW MARKET EXPECTATIONS]

The market-price burden of proof
is more important
than any outside target.

At the sourced ten-percent WACC,
reaching three hundred thirteen dollars
and four cents
requires adding
thirty-two-point-two-one-five
percentage points
to every annual
revenue-growth assumption.

Holding operations fixed instead
requires a
four-point-eight-seven-one-percent WACC.

Gross margin alone
cannot reach the price
below the ninety-nine-percent
test ceiling.

Even an aggressive bounded case
reaches only
two hundred three dollars
and ninety-five cents.

//

[SHOW SEMICONDUCTOR REGIMES]

I also connect
the semiconductor boom
to both operations
and market risk.

The extended AI-buildout case
raises growth and margins,
but beta rises to one-point-three-five
and WACC to ten-point-three-one-nine percent.

Value reaches
ninety-three dollars
and forty-eight cents.

Orderly normalization produces
eighty-three dollars
and twenty cents.

A cycle reversal combines
weaker growth and margins
with a one-point-five-zero beta,
producing fifty-four dollars
and sixty-nine cents
and a DO-NOT-INITIATE action.

Better demand
does not automatically mean
a lower discount rate.

//

[SHOW HEXAGON OVERLAY]

The Hexagon acquisition
stays separate
from the base statements.

Cadence paid
three-point-one-zero-one billion dollars
of consideration,
or two-point-nine-four-six billion
net of acquired cash.

Under the displayed assumptions,
the acquired operation is worth
one-point-zero-nine-five billion dollars,

and the combined illustration
is seventy-eight dollars
and ninety-three cents per share—

four dollars and forty-one cents
below the standalone base.

This does not prove
the transaction will destroy value.

Rather,
it shows that strategic fit alone
is not enough
to assume value creation.

//

[SHOW REVERSAL RULES]

I would reverse the decision
only when an acquisition-aware D-C-F
and at least two qualified peers
independently support value
at least fifteen percent
above the then-current market price.

I would stop
if the statements fail to tie,
a bridge is incomplete,
or terminal growth reaches WACC.

//

[CAMERA — COMMITTEE ASK]

My committee ask is to approve
WATCH AND DEFER,
with zero allocation,
at three hundred thirteen dollars
and four cents.

Assign me to refresh
the D-C-F,
peer set,
acquisition bridge,
and WACC
after the next two quarterly filings.

I will return only with
reconciled cash flows,
current peer evidence,
and a price that clears
the fifteen-percent
margin-of-safety rule.
