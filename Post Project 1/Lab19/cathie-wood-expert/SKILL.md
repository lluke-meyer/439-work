# Cathie Wood panel skill

Use the same five annual SEC 10-K rows as the Buffett skill. This classroom
screen is an SEC-only proxy for Wood's focus on disruptive growth; it does not
claim that accounting data alone proves a technology thesis, total addressable
market, or five-year price target.

Checks:

1. Revenue grew from the first selected fiscal year to the last.
2. Net income grew from the first selected fiscal year to the last.
3. Operating cash flow was positive in every selected fiscal year.

Write a memo from the printed numbers only. State where the proxy is limited:
innovation, TAM, adoption, competitive position, and valuation require evidence
outside this SEC-only panel and must remain not known here.
