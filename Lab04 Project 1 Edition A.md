### Operating performance and business mix

Total revenue increased from $4,089.986 million in fiscal 2023 to $4,641.264 million in fiscal 2024 and $5,296.759 million in fiscal 2025. This represents growth of approximately 13.5% in 2024 and 14.1% in 2025. Product and maintenance revenue was $3,834.359 million, $4,213.509 million, and $4,821.589 million, respectively. Services revenue was $255.627 million, $427.755 million, and $475.170 million.

The revenue trend is encouraging, but the mix still matters. Product and maintenance revenue represented approximately 91% of fiscal 2025 revenue. Cadence states that revenue from certain hardware, IP, and software products is recognized up-front, so reported growth should not automatically be treated as equivalent to recurring cash revenue.

Operating income increased from $1,251.225 million in 2023 to $1,350.763 million in 2024 and $1,492.042 million in 2025. Operating margins were approximately 30.6%, 29.1%, and 28.2%. The decline in reported operating margin deserves attention because research and development, marketing and sales, and acquisition-related amortization increased as the company expanded.

The first quarter of 2026 was stronger year over year. Revenue was $1,474.220 million versus $1,242.366 million in the first quarter of 2025, an increase of approximately 18.7%. Product and maintenance revenue increased to $1,348.922 million from $1,110.850 million, while services revenue declined to $125.298 million from $131.516 million. First-quarter operating income was $431.329 million versus $361.520 million, and net income was $335.660 million versus $273.579 million. The quarter’s operating margin was approximately 29.3%.

### Diluted shares and earnings per share

Weighted-average diluted shares were 272.748 million in 2023, 273.833 million in 2024, and 273.312 million in 2025. Diluted EPS was $3.82, $3.85, and $4.06. For the first quarter of 2026, weighted-average diluted shares were 273.725 million and diluted EPS was $1.23.

The March 2026 share count is particularly relevant because Cadence issued 3.2 million shares as part of the Hexagon Design and Engineering business acquisition. For a September 1, 2026 valuation, I should use a current diluted share convention rather than relying only on the 2025 annual weighted average.

### Cash, debt, and debt-like claims

Cash and cash equivalents were $1,008.152 million at December 31, 2023, $2,644.030 million at December 31, 2024, and $3,001.317 million at December 31, 2025. As of March 31, 2026, cash had declined to $1,406.668 million.

Debt changed substantially. At December 31, 2023, Cadence reported $349.285 million of current long-term debt and $299.771 million of long-term debt. At December 31, 2024, it reported $2,476.183 million of long-term debt and no current portion. At December 31, 2025, long-term debt was $2,480.150 million. By March 31, 2026, Cadence reported $425.000 million drawn on its revolving credit facility and $2,481.170 million of long-term debt, for total debt carrying value of approximately $2,906.170 million.

Operating lease liabilities remain a policy choice in the bridge. The 2025 filing disclosed total operating lease liabilities of $186.178 million, including $49.889 million current and $136.289 million long-term. The March 2026 filing disclosed $177.184 million of long-term operating lease liabilities; the current portion should be confirmed before using a total lease adjustment.

Cadence reported no preferred stock outstanding in the annual balance sheets, so preferred-stock claims are $0. Other liabilities should not automatically be treated as debt: the March 2026 balance sheet includes deferred tax liabilities and other operating liabilities that require separate analysis.

### Acquisition and balance-sheet implications

On February 23, 2026, Cadence completed its acquisition of Hexagon Smart Solutions AB’s design and engineering business. Consideration was $3.101 billion, consisting of $2.2 billion of cash and 3.2 million Cadence shares valued at $902.2 million. Cadence also completed two other first-quarter 2026 business combinations for aggregate cash consideration of $57.3 million, net of cash acquired.

After the acquisition, goodwill increased from $2,749.143 million at December 31, 2025 to $4,929.581 million at March 31, 2026, and acquired intangibles increased from $718.223 million to $1,933.262 million. The purchase-price allocation was preliminary as of March 31, 2026. The transaction may strengthen Cadence’s long-term system-design strategy, but it also increases integration risk, amortization, leverage, and the amount of future cash flow needed to justify the purchase price.

## Cadence filing-based enterprise-to-equity bridge

The SEC filings do not state enterprise value. Using filing data in millions:

Equity value = Enterprise value + cash − debt − debt-like claims − non-common claims

### December 31, 2025 bridge

Excluding operating leases:

Equity value = EV + $3,001.317M − $2,480.150M = EV + $521.167M

Per-share value = (EV + $521.167M) / 273.312M diluted shares

Treating the 2025 operating lease liability as debt-like:

Equity value = EV + $3,001.317M − $2,480.150M − $186.178M = EV + $334.989M

Per-share value = (EV + $334.989M) / 273.312M diluted shares

### March 31, 2026 updated bridge inputs

- Cash and cash equivalents: $1,406.668 million
- Revolving credit facility: $425.000 million
- Long-term debt: $2,481.170 million
- Total debt carrying value: approximately $2,906.170 million
- Weighted-average diluted shares for Q1 2026: 273.725 million
- Long-term operating lease liabilities: $177.184 million; current portion still to be confirmed

Using only cash and total debt, and excluding operating leases and other claims:

Equity value = EV + $1,406.668M − $2,906.170M = EV − $1,499.502M

Per-share value = (EV − $1,499.502M) / 273.725M diluted shares

This updated bridge is more conservative because the Hexagon acquisition used substantial cash and was partly financed with new revolver borrowings. Using December 2025 cash and debt after March 2026 would overstate the equity bridge.

## Research plan

- Determine Cadence’s market capitalization and share price as of September 1, 2026.
- Update the equity bridge using March 31, 2026 cash, total debt, current diluted shares, and a clearly stated lease policy.
- Confirm the current portion of operating lease liabilities and evaluate whether other claims should be included.
- Separate organic growth from acquisition-related growth, especially after the Hexagon transaction.
- Review recurring versus up-front revenue, remaining performance obligations, operating income, D&A, capex, and working capital.
- Build and validate an FCFF-based valuation reflecting acquisition-related amortization, integration costs, and financing changes.
- Test the valuation under different growth, margin, cost-of-capital, and terminal-growth assumptions.
- Compare the intrinsic valuation with relevant market multiples and Cadence’s historical trading range.
- Document whether the initial thesis should be maintained, revised, or rejected.
